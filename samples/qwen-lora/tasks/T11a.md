# Task T11a: the T11 documents (no code)

T11 is the unit `QwenModel` (the whole `qwen35` model for logits: token
embedding, the trunk blocks as `Recompute` stages on up to two devices, the
final norm and the head), the unit `LogprobsCli` and the program
`qlora_logprobs` (the next-token log-probabilities of H43). It is split
into T11a (documents), T11b1 to T11b3 (the units and their UT, one stage
each) and T11c (the ITa run). This is T11a. It uses **2 delegations**.

Read `{proj}/tasks/T11-facts.md` first: it fixes the interfaces, the
implementation, the messages, the UT rows, the ITa and the case-ID ranges.
The documents only restate and organise it. Do not read DESIGN.md, other
task files or any source under `src/` or `tests/`.

Main agent (you):
1. Write `{proj}/docs/external-design/T11-external.md` (purpose of the
   units and the program, the forward from token ids to logits step by
   step, the tied head, the trunk count without the MTP block, the device
   split, the command line, its input and output format and exit codes, the
   errors, as in the facts; H43 and why the reference is an all-F32 copy
   with an f32 KV cache) and `{proj}/docs/system-flow/T11-system.md`
   (callers: T17's training runs `Embed`, the stages with T13's
   `Recompute` and `Logits`; `qlora_logprobs` is H43's tool here and at
   M4 on the 27B; the UT compares with the scalar `RefLogits` of
   `tests/ut/QwenRefScalar.pas` on the tiny models of
   `tests/ut/QwenBlocksFixture.pas`; the ITa driver
   `tests/ita/T11/logprobs_check.pas` compares `qlora_logprobs` with the
   reference of `work/oracle/T11`).
